## Reveal schedule
- Depth 1: Acts I onward, everyday hearsay; never present guesses as established truth.
- Depth 2: Late Act I onward, with a relevant discovered witness or item.
- Depth 3: Act II onward, after the corresponding investigation has exposed contradictory evidence.
- Depth 4: Act III onward, with the affected character's consent or a credible record.
- Depth 5: Endgame only, tied to the applicable ambition finale; never spoil with ordinary encounter cards.
- Competing accounts must have explicit *rumour*, *unreliable witness*, or *superseded* annotations until the writing lead resolves the contradiction. Six deliberately opposed account pairs are reserved for editorial review.
