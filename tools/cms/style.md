# Narrative studio — house style
This guide applies to both games, interpreted through each game's own Bible and Quality registry.

## Sentence rhythm
Short sentences reveal impact; longer, deliberate sentences build texture or bureaucratic absurdity.

- Flat: “The room is ominous.” / On brand: “The drawer bears your name. The key does not fit.”
- Flat: “It is a very long queue.” / On brand: “At the counter, a clerk asks you to prove you have waited. The queue behind you is evidence enough.”
- Flat: “Someone is sad.” / On brand: “She folds the letter along the same worn crease, as though the answer might change if it is small enough.”

## Imagery
Prefer chalk, rust, brass, wet paper, stamps, cold lamps, tannin, damp coats, mechanical rhythms and missing spaces in a familiar street. Bright colour should be rare and meaningful. Avoid generic fantasy magic beams, tropical landscapes, ornamental gore and interchangeable fog.

## Dialogue
NPCs possess occupations, stakes, evasions and rhythms. Some are tired, some are sharp or playful. Don't write everyone as an ancient oracle. Prefer “The clerk smooths the form. ‘The dead are not available for comment.’” to “she said angrily”. Speech earns its peculiar vocabulary through context.

## Dry humour
- “The appeal is permitted, provided it is made yesterday.”
- “Your signature is legible. This counts against you.”
- “The committee agrees to meet when the meetings stop.”
- “The ferryman can waive the fare. He cannot waive the crossing.”

## Second person
Describe observation and action; do not assume the player's feelings, faith, gender or moral verdict. “You see a mark beneath the seal” leaves agency intact. Avoid “You are the only hero brave enough to understand.”

## Forbidden constructions
1. “Little did you know …”
2. “Suddenly, without warning …”
3. “You can't help but feel …”
4. “A chill runs down your spine.”
5. “The fate of the world rests in your hands.”
6. “Everything changes forever.”
7. “Only time will tell.”
8. “A mysterious figure appears.”
9. “You have proven yourself worthy.”
10. “The silence is deafening.”

## Three passes
1. **Experience** — specific setting, readable scene, character motivation, distinct consequence and no empty adjectives.
2. **Continuity** — secret depth, timeline, prior NPC commitments and distinctions between a rumour and a confirmed fact.
3. **Mechanics** — correct stats, rarity, progress, resources, failure handling, action cost, rare roll and testable route.

## Independent AI stages
An AI may pitch, structure, draft, copy-edit, challenge economy assumptions, validate references and identify apparent contradictions. AI never self-signs peer review. Check the prose against game canon and record the human decision before publishing.
