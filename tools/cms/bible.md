# NeathBound — Project Bible (authoring edition)
> **Status:** Source-grounded summary with proposed details clearly marked. Existing shipped stories and the game rules remain authoritative when they disagree with a proposed idea. Review before canonising.

## Premise
You awaken as **The Unmoored** in a municipal chamber whose iron door carries the wrong name. Above you hangs a sunless city of lamps, drowned streets, locked records and exchanges made in memories. The bell stamped on your cell returns in places no sane cartographer has connected.

The institutions that keep the city alive also control what it remembers. Lamplighters manage the dark, archivists keep dangerous papers, ferrymen cross compromised waters, and brokers trade in the missing portions of other people's accounts. Below the public map lie Ashrail Station, Cinder Ward and the Registry of Hours, places whose records fail their inhabitants.

Your earliest inquiry, **The Missing Name**, asks why official memory no longer agrees with your own past. Later vows investigate the source of light, the river's unpaid promises, the Registry's extra hour, and the Spire's hunted Gloomwing. The player's decisions should be remembered by people, not merely by a number.

## Tonal rules
1. Address the player as *you*, in present tense.
2. Gothic urban textures: iron, damp chalk, archive paste, cold lamps.
3. Melancholy must arise from choices and costs, not endless adjectives.
4. Allow dry humour in bureaucracy and mistaken certainty.
5. An NPC wants something comprehensible, however strange its means.
6. Never congratulate the player for a choice; show a consequence.
7. Prefer specific sensory evidence over omniscient exposition.
8. A triumph costs an individual, a relationship or an institution something.
9. Separate testimony, rumour and confirmed fact.
10. Leave space for tenderness without sentimental declarations.

## Dispositions and opposing tendencies
The running engine has opposites **Austere/Hedonist, Magnanimous/Ruthless, Steadfast/Fickle, Honest/Devious** and a triangle of **Daring/Forceful/Subtle**. These eleven labels, not an invented nine-quirk replacement, are the source registry. Disposition ranks are capped at 15 and CP opposition must be respected.

## Circles of influence
- **Lamplighters**: civic safety, paid darkness control; barter light, licences and testimony.
- **Archivists**: preservation with compromised neutrality; offer access, records and inconvenient truths.
- **Ferrymen**: custodians of dangerous crossings; offer routes, transport and obligations.
- **Velvet Houses**: patronage that often comes with hidden conditions; trade introductions and protection.
- **Brass Union**: legal arrangements, labour and enforceable debts; trade warrants and contracts.
- **Roof Surveyors**: dangerous topography and the city's upper reaches; trade maps and sightings.

## Central mystery
**Established premise:** the Registry's records, the bell and the loss of the player's name are connected. **Proposed long-arc answer, not yet canon:** the city's institutions learned to transfer memories and elapsed hours between people, then hid the cost in falsified records. The deepest ledger entries remain proposals until approved by continuity review.

## Forbidden imports from reference works
Do not lift proprietary names, named character designs, lore or familiar scenes from other narrative RPGs. In new original NeathBound prose specifically avoid the stat names **Watchful, Shadowy, Dangerous, Persuasive** and the phrase **Fallen London**. Rules may be inspired by the quality-based narrative format; prose must be independently authored.
